## The A/B

Three photos and three sketch results, each built two ways: (A) the single image, (B) the original plus the planned extensions as a multi-image prompt. That is six worlds and, at the recorded prices (`docs/spikes/1.1.md`), about 230 credits each on the draft model or about 1,580 each on the standard one. The cheap way is the draft model for all six: 1,380 credits. Add the outpaint calls: 2 views for each B photo, so 6 image units.

## Before anything is sent

1. **The multi-image request shape is unconfirmed.** `steering/worldlabs-api.md` names `type: "multi-image"` only. Read the service's page for how each image carries its azimuth, then write the adapter (`HttpWorldLabs` sends one image today) and put the shape in the steering file. Until then option B cannot be made at all, and this is the blocker.
2. **Whether the image model can continue a picture sideways** is unknown: the documented form has no mask input, and a reference image must be under 512 by 512. Try it first with `ai-spike` (6 units) and look at the six extensions before spending any credits on worlds.
3. Record per world: credits, seconds, and your preference (A, B, no difference) with one line of why. Decide the `ENHANCE_VIEWS` default and whether `ENHANCE_ENABLED` ever goes on.
