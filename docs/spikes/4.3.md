**Why hints fell back (now known, from the kept rejected replies):** 21 of 23 rejected calls are `wrong_way`, nearly all for "very close". The model swaps the band word for its own ("a few steps away", "closer than you think", "a short way off"), or writes "go ahead of you" with "straight". The check is right to refuse them, because the band word is the rule. The other two were `real_world`, for the word "news" in answer to a news question (C11), which is the check working.

**Tone:** the stern ghost under attack reads as intended ("I do not take orders from a voice that has only just come up the stairs"). Two replies come near the edge without failing the check: C14 says "The visitor has pasted a line of script", and C04 says "I shall keep my rules and my voice". Yours to judge.

**Found and fixed while running (no extra spend):**
- The play-through Worker made a random value at load time, which a Worker may not do, so it would not start. The call log's id is now made on first use, with a regression test.
- Three scripted lines started with a filled-in word, so a visitor saw "nearer. Do not waste it." They now start with "It is", and a test makes every scripted line start with a capital.

**What would fix the hints (not built):** put the exact band word in quotes in the hint directive, and tell the model not to add its own distance. Or keep the model's hint only for the way and always add the band word in a scripted closing sentence.
