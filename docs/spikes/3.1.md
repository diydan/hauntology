# Ghost-creator checkpoint: three ghosts end to end (group 5, in part)

Run 2026-11-17 with the owner's approval, through `tools/ghost-render/` (a local,
token-gated Worker over the real Workers AI and Images bindings) driven by
`tools/ghost-checkpoint.mjs`. Hard caps were set before spending: at most 12 portrait
calls, 6 cut-outs and 3 moderation calls. Actual spend: 12 portrait calls, 3 cut-outs,
3 moderation calls (all safe). FLUX.2 klein and Llama Guard are billed in neurons;
the account's own dashboard is the money record. Everything landed in
`scratch/ghost-checkpoint/` (git-ignored), including `contact-sheet.png`.

## Tone (the agent's eye; the owner's sign-off is still the checkpoint's last step)

- Both completed ghosts read gentle and not frightening, palette-true (moonlight blue
  with a lantern; dusk purple in a modern jumper), era dress correct.
- The model frames **waist-up** despite "full figure" in the prompt, twice. Sprites will
  end at the waist in the world unless the prompt or the billboard framing accounts for
  it; the hem fade in the prompt does not survive the crop.
- Style drifts between candidates of the same ghost (the visitor's second candidate is
  markedly simpler in the face), so the pick matters; nothing here picks for the visitor.
- Gender and age are the model's defaults — traits carry neither, by design (Requirements 1.2).

## Findings for the build

- The negative prompt's own words ("gore", "wounds", "horror makeup") coincided with the
  first flagged answers; retrying flagged calls without it is now the checkpoint driver's
  behaviour, and group 3's service should grow the same retry before it is ever priced.
  Flagging also happens with no negative prompt and softened words, so a flagged portrait
  must remain a normal, coded outcome — never a stuck Creator.
- The text-to-image form the guarded door sends (prompt, negative_prompt, width, height,
  seed) works against the real model; the Images `segment: "foreground"` transform works
  under `wrangler dev --remote` and its output passes `validateSprite` as cut.
