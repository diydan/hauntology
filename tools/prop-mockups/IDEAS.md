# Prop ideas

Status on 2026-11-15. Mockups for the first seven are in `mockups/`. Everything else waits until the game runs end to end. Then we review every mechanical (servo) and static (printed, painted, LED-only) prop that fits.

Every prop follows one rule: it reacts only to what the Display shows (the public log and the Public View). Never a Hand, an Inspect or Eavesdrop result, a sealed Accusation's content, a Sight, a Lock answer, or who sent a Rattle, Cue or Frame.

## Parked: review once the game runs (added 2026-11-15)

| Prop | Fires on (public) | How often | Parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resolve lamps: three lights on each seat's base | Resolve changes (+1 each Round; Help, Spook and Eavesdrop spend it) | Every Round, every seat | 18 LED pixels on one data pin, no servo |
| Hour bell: a real bell struck by a servo hammer | The hour strike (12 at midnight) and the bell Cue | An hour strike about once an hour; Cues up to 6 per Turn | 1 servo, 1 bell |
| Seat shiver: the pawn trembles | A Frame or House Cue names that player's phone | At most 1 per sender per Round | None extra if #5 is built (reuses its six servos) |
| Blackout lamp: a real lamp dims slowly | The Blackout Haunt Event | About once a game | 1 LED lamp, or 1 servo flap |

Also parked: let the chandelier (#12) answer the creak Cue as well as Rattles, with no new parts.

Spec anchors: party-mode Public View carries Resolve counts; the hour strike and midnight Bell; a Frame is logged as a House Cue naming only the target's phone and the sender is revealed only at the ending; Blackout is one of the four Haunt Events.
