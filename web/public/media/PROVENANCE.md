# Hero background media provenance

The record for every clip in this folder, and the practice from Requirement 18 of `.kiro/specs/puzzles` followed for
`tools/playtest/media`: a source record, one row per clip, and a flash check. Requirement 18 itself governs the
public-domain footage used as puzzle shots and does not cover these files, which are generated from the project's own
artwork and are not shots of any puzzle scene. The practice is kept anyway so the folder ships the same way as the rest
of the media in this repo.

These files are the animated hero background of the web app. Nothing in `web/src` points at them yet, and the still
they were made from is not in this repository either, so the clips and their poster frames are the only copy of the hero
picture the app can serve. Wiring them into the hero is left to whoever picks the work up.

### What the model adds to the source

The video models burn a `Generated by MiniMax AI` watermark into every frame. The bottom 62 pixels are cropped away on
every clip below, which is why the frames are 706 tall and not 768. The crop removes the foreground grass and the feet
of the nearest gravestones; the composition is otherwise untouched. `object-fit: cover` in the page absorbs the change of
aspect.

The model also drifts the camera a little even when the brief asks for a locked-off shot, and keeps the moon up after
sunrise. Both are visible in the rows below and neither is corrected by a cut.

## Open points

- The origin of `HauntologyHeroBG.jpg` is not written down anywhere in this repo. It is the project's own artwork as far
  as this folder knows, and nothing here records who drew it or under what terms. Someone should write that down before
  the game is sold.
- The still `HauntologyHeroBG.jpg` is not in this repository. Every clip here was made from it, but the file itself
  lives outside the repo, so the poster frames are the only hero still that ships with the app.
- The model drifts the camera in the dawn clip and keeps the moon visible after sunrise. Both are model behaviour, not
  cuts, and both are visible on screen.
- The dawn clips are 15 s and the night clip is 9.3 s, which is well over the 5 s ceiling Requirement 18 puts on a
  puzzle shot. That ceiling does not apply to a hero background, but a clip from this folder must never be reused as a
  puzzle shot without being re-cut.
