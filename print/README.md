# Hauntology 3D Print Kit & Tabletop Props (`print/`)

> **Printing is 100% optional.** Every Hauntology Party Mode room, solo case, Suspect selection, Relic selection, and Display mode works in full on phones and screens alone without printing any 3D piece or building any hardware prop (Requirement 8.5). When you do print the kit, the pieces bring tactile Victorian séance miniatures, scannable `hw:r1`–`hw:r6` Relic tokens, a Pepper's-ghost pyramid frame, Suspect standees, and seven ESP32 + SG90 tabletop props to the table.

---

## 1. Ground Rules, Slicer Settings & Reference Hardware

Every model in `print/scad/` and `print/stl/` is authored to print cleanly on a single-extruder FDM printer (such as a Bambu Lab A1 Mini, Prusa MINI+, or Ender 3):

- **Build Volume:** Every piece fits comfortably inside a **`180 × 180 mm`** bed (`<= 174 mm` longest dimension).
- **Nozzle & Layer Height:** `0.4 mm` nozzle at **`0.20 mm`** standard layer height (`0.16 mm` optional for the 6 Relic miniatures).
- **Walls & Infill:** `3` perimeter walls (`>= 1.6 mm` minimum wall thickness across all structural models), `15%` gyroid or grid infill, `4` top / `4` bottom solid layers.
- **Supports:** every single piece across all **42 STLs prints 100% support-free** (`0` parts need supports). `node tools/build-print.mjs --check` enforces it: no ceiling may be a flat roof wider than `15 mm`, nothing may face down steeper than `50°` from vertical (the design target is `45°`), and the README must stay in exact sync with the validator. Every part has `>= 100 mm²` flat bed contact at `z = 0`.
- **Moving Clearances:** `0.30 mm` (`tol`) radial/sliding clearance on all hinges, levers, dovetails, and snap sockets; `0.15 mm` (`clear_fit`) tight clearance on SG90 servo spline hubs and single-arm horn recesses.
- **One Solid Spool Colour per STL:** No multi-material AMS/MMU required. All lettering, dial ticks, portraits, and resistor bands live on the **1:1 scale printable SVG sticker sheets** in [`print/stickers/`](./stickers/), which sit inside **`0.4 mm` recessed pockets** on the 3D prints so sticker edges never peel.
- **Reference Hardware (`scad/_common.scad`):**
  - **Micro Servo:** Reference **`TowerPro SG90 9g`** (`22.8 × 12.2 × 22.5 mm` body, `32.5 × 2.5 mm` mounting ears at `16.0 mm` from bottom, `27.5 mm` M2 screw hole centres, `Ø12.0 × 4.0 mm` output boss, `Ø4.8 mm` spline shaft, `19.5 mm` single-arm horn). *Note:* Clone SG90/MG90S servos vary by `±0.5 mm` in ear height and body length — measure your servos with calipers before printing all enclosures and tweak `sg90_*` in [`scad/_common.scad`](./scad/_common.scad) if needed.
  - **Microcontroller:** Reference **`Espressif ESP32-WROOM-32E DevKitC V4`** (`55.0 × 28.0 mm` PCB footprint, `56.0 × 29.0 mm` pocket with `0.5 mm` clearance per side, `4×` M2 corner standoffs on a `48.26 × 22.86 mm` rectangle (`1.90 × 0.90 in`), and a `18 × 10 mm` PCB trace-antenna keep-out window at the opposite end from the USB-C port). All 5 electronic props are powered via their rear USB-C port (`5 V` USB power bank or wall adapter; no internal LiPo battery bay).
- **Validation Command:** Run `node tools/build-print.mjs --check` at any time to verify that all **42 STL files** in `print/stl/` and `dist/print/` are binary STLs, watertight 2-manifolds (`0` open/non-manifold edges, `0` degenerate triangles), exact expected connected-component counts (`1` solid for all single-piece parts, `10` for `prop-20-plug-heads.stl`, `12` for `prop-20-terminal-blocks.stl`), `>= 100 mm²` flat bed contact at `z = 0`, positive volume, and within bed bounds.

## 4. Design & Mechanism Notes (Addressing `3D_BRIEF.md` & Mechanical Review)

1. **Relic Centre Connector with No Base Plates (`relic-*.stl` + `relic-token-pocket.stl`)**
   - None of the 6 Relic miniatures (`r1`–`r6`) carry a `33 mm` circular base plate. Each Relic has a flat `Ø14.6 mm` foot (`> 100 mm²` of bed contact at `z = 0`, a short 45° corbel into the sculpture) with a `Ø6.1 × 3.3 mm` socket in the middle, and it drops onto the **`Ø5.6 mm` peg** in the middle of `relic-token-pocket.stl`. The peg is small on purpose: the `hw:r1`–`hw:r6` QR codes are Version 1, error level Q (25%), and a `Ø6 mm` hole punched in the middle of the `36 × 36 mm` sticker still scans (tested with OpenCV: 6 of 6 at 6, 6.5 and 7 mm, even 0.6 mm off centre; 0 of 6 with the earlier `Ø14 mm` hole). A mounted Relic covers the sticker, so lift it off to scan.
2. **Prop #1 (`prop-01-ghost-in-box`): Coaxial Hinge + `-Y`-Shaft SG90 `XZ` Pop-Up**
   - `prop-01-chest-body.stl` mounts its SG90 horizontally on an internal pedestal at `X = -43, Y = +16.75` (`3.5 mm` front wall), with the output spline pointing along **`-Y`** at `X = -48.5, Z = 32.0 mm` so `prop-01-lever-arm.stl` sweeps vertically in the **`XZ` plane** (`Y = -11.0 .. -6.5 mm`), cleanly in front of the ghost side-edge U-tracks (`X = ±36.15, Y = -4.9 .. +2.7 mm`). Its Stage-1 cam nose pushes the underside lid ramp while its `4.4 mm` slotted fork lifts `prop-01-ghost-figure.stl`'s `-Y` follower pin. When `prop-01-chest-lid.stl` is flipped right-way up (`FLIP = rotate([180,0,0])`), both hinge knuckle axes sit coaxially behind the rear wall at `Y = +51.2, Z = 69.0 mm` with `0.30 mm` (`clear_move`) rim clearance (`Z_lid_rim = 70.3 mm`) and full closed headroom above the ghost.
3. **Prop #3 (`prop-03-haunt-needle`): Through-Bored SG90 Boss + Support-Free 45° Vaulted ESP32 Bay**
   - `prop-03-dial-plate.stl` (`26.4 mm` body thickness) counterbores the central hub to `Ø12.4 mm` (`sg90_boss_d`) all the way through the front wall and seats the SG90 ears at `Z = 18.9 mm`, so the servo back stays cleanly inside `Z >= 2.9 mm` while the spline shaft protrudes `5.5 mm` past the front dial face (`Z = 31.9 mm`). The lower tabletop base plinth (`Y = -26..-16 mm`) sits flush behind the `Z = 26.4 mm` front face so it never collides with `prop-03-bezel-ring.stl` or `prop-03-needle.stl`, and the rear ESP32 bay uses `45°` vaulted walls converging to a `4.5 mm` ridge at `Z = 24.0 mm` so the entire part prints 100% support-free. `prop-03-needle.stl` trims its counterweight radius to `9.6 mm` (`< 10.0 mm` lower bezel viewport opening) alongside a keyed single-arm SG90 horn cavity (`clear_fit = 0.15 mm`) plus M2 screw bores, and `prop-03-max-tab.stl` clips radially into the dial rim detents while stepping cleanly over the bezel at `Z >= 31.0 mm`.
4. **Prop #5 (`prop-05-haunting-flipper`): Exact `100.0 mm` Modular Pitch + Open Pawn Socket & Diffuser Slot**
