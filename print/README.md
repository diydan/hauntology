# Hauntology 3D Print Kit & Tabletop Props (`print/`)

> **Printing is 100% optional.** Every Hauntology Party Mode room, solo case, Suspect selection, Relic selection, and Display mode works in full on phones and screens alone without printing any 3D piece or building any hardware prop (Requirement 8.5). When you do print the kit, the pieces bring tactile Victorian séance miniatures, scannable `hw:r1`–`hw:r6` Relic tokens, a Pepper's-ghost pyramid frame, Suspect standees, and seven ESP32 + SG90 tabletop props to the table.

---

## 1. Ground Rules, Slicer Settings & Reference Hardware

Every model in `print/scad/` and `print/stl/` is authored to print cleanly on a single-extruder FDM printer (such as a Bambu Lab A1 Mini, Prusa MINI+, or Ender 3):

- **Build Volume:** Every piece fits inside a **`180 × 180 mm`** bed (`<= 177.8 mm` longest dimension).
- **Nozzle & Layer Height:** `0.4 mm` nozzle at **`0.20 mm`** standard layer height (`0.16 mm` optional for the 6 Relic miniatures).
- **Walls & Infill:** `3` perimeter walls (`>= 1.6 mm` minimum wall thickness across all structural models), `15%` gyroid or grid infill, `4` top / `4` bottom solid layers.
- **Supports:** every single piece across all **45 STLs prints 100% support-free** (`0` parts need supports). `node tools/build-print.mjs --check` enforces it: no ceiling may be a flat roof wider than `15 mm`, nothing may face down steeper than `50°` from vertical (the design target is `45°`), and the README must stay in exact sync with the validator. Every part has `>= 100 mm²` flat bed contact at `z = 0`.
- **Moving Clearances:** `0.30 mm` (`tol`) radial/sliding clearance on all hinges, levers, dovetails, and snap sockets; `0.15 mm` (`clear_fit`) tight clearance on SG90 servo spline hubs and single-arm horn recesses.
- **One Solid Spool Colour per STL:** No multi-material AMS/MMU required. All lettering, dial ticks, portraits, and resistor bands live on the **1:1 scale printable SVG sticker sheets** in [`print/stickers/`](./stickers/), which sit inside **`0.4 mm` recessed pockets** on the 3D prints so sticker edges never peel.
- **Reference Hardware (`scad/_common.scad`):**
  - **Micro Servo:** Reference **`TowerPro SG90 9g`** (`22.8 × 12.2 × 22.5 mm` body, `32.5 × 2.5 mm` mounting ears at `16.0 mm` from bottom, `27.5 mm` M2 screw hole centres, `Ø12.0 × 4.0 mm` output boss, `Ø4.8 mm` spline shaft, `19.5 mm` single-arm horn). *Note:* Clone SG90/MG90S servos vary by `±0.5 mm` in ear height and body length — measure your servos with calipers before printing all enclosures and tweak `sg90_*` in [`scad/_common.scad`](./scad/_common.scad) if needed.
  - **Microcontroller:** Reference **`Espressif ESP32-WROOM-32E DevKitC V4`** (`55.0 × 28.0 mm` PCB footprint, `56.0 × 29.0 mm` pocket with `0.5 mm` clearance per side, `4×` M2 corner standoffs on a `48.26 × 22.86 mm` rectangle (`1.90 × 0.90 in`), and a `18 × 10 mm` PCB trace-antenna keep-out window at the opposite end from the USB-C port). All 5 electronic props are powered via their rear USB-C port (`5 V` USB power bank or wall adapter; no internal LiPo battery bay).
- **Validation Command:** Run `node tools/build-print.mjs --check` at any time to verify that all **45 STL files** in `print/stl/` and `dist/print/` are binary STLs, watertight 2-manifolds (`0` open/non-manifold edges, `0` degenerate triangles), exact expected connected-component counts (`1` solid for all single-piece parts, `10` for `prop-20-plug-heads.stl`, `12` for `prop-20-terminal-blocks.stl`), `>= 100 mm²` flat bed contact at `z = 0`, positive volume, and within bed bounds.
- **Assembly Collision Checks:** `node tools/check-print-assembly.mjs` (needs OpenSCAD, about `30` seconds) puts the compiled STLs in the poses of the finished props (`#1`, `#3`, `#5`, `#9`, `#15`: closed lids, servo seats, flaps, the curtain's panels at six positions, the latch arm turned through `180°`, the curtain's threads) and intersects them: `117` checks must overlap nothing, and `25` controls (a part moved on purpose) must collide, so a wrong pose cannot pass quietly. `--only p09,p15` runs some props, `--list` lists the checks, and `--route` prints the curtain's thread route (lengths, wraps, elastic stretch) without OpenSCAD. The thread route is worked out in `tools/lib/print-route.mjs` from the SCAD files' own numbers. Servo and horn sizes in the poses are an `SG90` from memory.

## 4. Design & Mechanism Notes (Addressing `3D_BRIEF.md` & Mechanical Review)

1. **Relic Centre Connector with No Base Plates (`relic-*.stl` + `relic-token-pocket.stl`)**
   - None of the 6 Relic miniatures (`r1`–`r6`) carry a `33 mm` circular base plate. Each Relic has a flat `Ø14.6 mm` foot (`> 100 mm²` of bed contact at `z = 0`, a short 45° corbel into the sculpture) with a `Ø6.1 × 3.3 mm` socket in the middle, and it drops onto the **`Ø5.6 mm` peg** in the middle of `relic-token-pocket.stl`. The peg is small on purpose: the `hw:r1`–`hw:r6` QR codes are Version 1, error level Q (25%), and a `Ø6 mm` hole punched in the middle of the `36 × 36 mm` sticker still scans (tested with OpenCV: 6 of 6 at 6, 6.5 and 7 mm, even 0.6 mm off centre; 0 of 6 with the earlier `Ø14 mm` hole). A mounted Relic covers the sticker, so lift it off to scan.
2. **Prop #1 (`prop-01-ghost-in-box`): Coaxial Hinge + `-Y`-Shaft SG90 `XZ` Pop-Up**
   - `prop-01-chest-body.stl` mounts its SG90 horizontally on an internal pedestal at `X = -43, Y = +16.75` (`3.5 mm` front wall, flange slot centered `5.25 mm` behind case front), with the output spline pointing along **`-Y`** at `X = -48.5, Z = 32.0 mm` so `prop-01-lever-arm.stl` sweeps vertically in the **`XZ` plane** (`Y = -11.0 .. -6.5 mm` arm, stepped `11.5 mm` horn boss reaching `Y = +0.5 mm` outside the left U-track), cleanly in front of the ghost side-edge U-tracks (`X = ±36.15, Y = -4.9 .. +2.7 mm`). Its Stage-1 cam nose pushes the underside lid ramp while its `4.4 mm` slotted fork lifts `prop-01-ghost-figure.stl`'s extended `-Y` follower pin (`Y_world = -15.6 mm`, clearing `0..6 mm` horn offsets). When `prop-01-chest-lid.stl` is flipped right-way up (`FLIP = rotate([180,0,0])`), both hinge knuckle axes sit coaxially behind the rear wall at `Y = +51.2, Z = 69.0 mm` with `0.30 mm` (`clear_move`) rim clearance (`Z_lid_rim = 70.3 mm`) and full closed headroom above the ghost.
3. **Prop #3 (`prop-03-haunt-needle`): Through-Bored SG90 Boss + Support-Free 45° Vaulted ESP32 Bay**
   - `prop-03-dial-plate.stl` (`26.4 mm` body thickness) counterbores the central hub to `Ø12.4 mm` (`sg90_boss_d`) all the way through the front wall and seats the SG90 ears at `Z = 18.9 mm` with a `23.8 mm` body pocket ceiling (`>= 0.9 mm` above the `22.9 mm` servo case top while `Z = 0.4 mm` keeps the back inside `Z >= 0` and the spline shaft protrudes `5.5 mm` past `Z = 26.4 mm`). ## 5. Pepper's-Ghost Acetate Cutting Guide (`pyramid-holder.stl`)

To complete the Pepper's-ghost display (`pyramid-holder.stl`), cut **four identical trapezoids** from `0.3 mm`–`0.5 mm` clear overhead-projector (OHP) acetate film or clear PETG sheet:

- **Bottom edge (`baseMm`):** `60.0 mm`
- **Top edge (`topMm`):** `10.0 mm`
- **Slant height (`slantHeightMm`, 45° wall angle):** `35.36 mm`
- **Vertical height (`verticalHeightMm`):** `25.0 mm`

Slide the four trapezoids into the `45°` corner channels of `pyramid-holder.stl` (a tiny dab of clear tape along the outside seam is optional) and rest the frame over the `/party/display` calibration square on a phone or tablet.
