# Hauntology 3D Print Kit & Tabletop Props (`print/`)

> **Printing is 100% optional.** Every Hauntology Party Mode room, solo case, Suspect selection, Relic selection, and Display mode works in full on phones and screens alone without printing any 3D piece or building any hardware prop (Requirement 8.5). When you do print the kit, the pieces bring tactile Victorian séance miniatures, scannable `hw:r1`–`hw:r6` Relic tokens, a Pepper's-ghost pyramid frame, Suspect standees, and three ESP32 + SG90 tabletop builds (a 4-lock chest, a clocktower stage and the #5 flipper) to the table.

---

## 1. Ground Rules, Slicer Settings & Reference Hardware

Every model in `print/scad/` and `print/stl/` is authored to print cleanly on a single-extruder FDM printer (such as a Bambu Lab A1 Mini, Prusa MINI+, or Ender 3):

- **Build Volume:** Every piece fits inside a **`180 × 180 mm`** bed (`<= 173.0 mm` longest dimension, leaving `>= 7.0 mm` skirt/margin).
- **Nozzle & Layer Height:** `0.4 mm` nozzle at **`0.20 mm`** standard layer height (`0.16 mm` optional for the 6 Relic miniatures). Enable a **`4–5 mm` outer brim** on tall narrow-footprint pieces (`standee-*.stl` and upright `relic-*.stl` miniatures on `Ø14.6 mm` feet).
- **Walls & Infill:** `3` perimeter walls (`>= 1.6 mm` minimum wall thickness across all structural models), `15%` gyroid or grid infill, `4` top / `4` bottom solid layers.
- **Supports:** every single piece across all **47 STLs prints 100% support-free** (`0` parts need supports). `node tools/build-print.mjs --check` enforces it: no ceiling (including low-Z bridges right off the bed) may be a flat roof wider than `10 mm`, nothing may face down steeper than `50.5°` from vertical (the design target is `45°`), and the README must stay in exact sync with the validator. Every part has `>= 100 mm²` flat bed contact at `z = 0`.
- **Moving Clearances:** `0.30 mm` (`tol`) radial/sliding clearance on all hinges, levers, dovetails, and snap sockets; `0.15 mm` (`clear_fit`) tight clearance on SG90 servo spline hubs and single-arm horn recesses; `1.80 mm` self-tapping `M2` pilot bores.
- **One Solid Spool Colour per STL:** No multi-material AMS/MMU required. All lettering, dial ticks, portraits, and resistor bands live on the **1:1 scale printable SVG sticker sheets** in [`print/stickers/`](./stickers/), which sit inside **`0.4 mm` recessed pockets** on the 3D prints so sticker edges never peel.
- **Reference Hardware (`scad/_common.scad`):**
  - **Micro Servo:** Reference **`TowerPro SG90 9g`** (`22.8 × 12.2 × 22.5 mm` body, `32.5 × 2.5 mm` mounting ears at `16.0 mm` from bottom, `27.5 mm` M2 screw hole centres, `Ø11.8 × 4.0 mm` output boss, `Ø4.8 mm` spline shaft, `19.5 mm` single-arm horn). *Note:* Clone SG90/MG90S servos vary by `±0.5 mm` in ear height and body length — measure your servos with calipers before printing all enclosures and tweak `sg90_*` in [`scad/_common.scad`](./scad/_common.scad) if needed.
  - **Microcontroller:** Reference **`Espressif ESP32-WROOM-32E DevKitC V4`** (`54.4 × 27.9 mm` PCB, two rows of 19 header pins `22.86 mm` apart, `55.6 × 28.5 mm` pocket). The chest, the stage and the #5 flipper each carry **one shared ESP32** and an external USB-C cable exit (`5 V` USB power bank or wall adapter; no internal LiPo bay). *Mounting:* a stock DevKitC V4 may have **no mounting holes** (the `4× M2` pilots in the SCAD sit at the corners of the header pattern, on the pins), so the bays hold the board on ledges or shelves, and you secure it with a dab of hot glue or tape. The chest bay holds the board flipped (module and USB down, header pins up); the stage's screen-bezel cradle holds it module-up with the pins in a relief below. Both clear a board with its header pins fitted (`check-print-assembly.mjs` tests this).
- **Validation Command:** Run `node tools/build-print.mjs --check` at any time to verify that all **47 STL files** in `print/stl/` and `dist/print/` are binary STLs, watertight 2-manifolds (`0` open/non-manifold edges, `0` degenerate triangles), exact expected connected-component counts (`1` solid for single-piece parts, `3` for `prop-09-rail-and-pulley.stl` and `prop-15-vault-clasp.stl`, `4` for `prop-12-bats-mobile.stl`, `10` for `prop-20-plug-heads.stl`, `6` for `prop-20-terminal-blocks.stl`), `>= 100 mm²` flat bed contact at `z = 0`, positive volume, support-free (`<= 10 mm` bridge span), and within bed bounds.
- **Assembly Collision Checks:** `node tools/check-print-assembly.mjs` (needs OpenSCAD, about `15–30` seconds with `--jobs 8`) puts the compiled STLs in the poses of the finished builds (`#1` chest, `#3` clocktower dial, `#5` flipper, `#9` stage, `#15` lockbox, plus `relic-token-pocket` + `relic-ghost` in the vault tray: closed & opening lids, servo seats, MAX tab, flaps, the curtain's panels at six positions, the latch arm turned through `180°`, the curtain's threads, and an ESP32 DevKitC with its header pins in the chest's rear bay and in the stage's screen-bezel cradle) and intersects them: `168` checks must overlap nothing, and `44` controls (`212` checks total) must collide, so a wrong pose cannot pass quietly. `--only p09,p15` runs some props, `--list` lists the checks, `--stl <dir>` checks STLs somewhere else, and `--route` prints the curtain's thread route (lengths, wraps, elastic stretch) without OpenSCAD. The thread route is worked out in `tools/lib/print-route.mjs` from the SCAD files' own numbers. Servo, horn and ESP32 sizes in the poses are from memory of the datasheets (SG90, DevKitC V4): measure your own.

## 4. Design & Mechanism Notes (Addressing `3D_BRIEF.md` & Mechanical Review)

> Items 2, 3, 6, 7 and 8 below were written for the standalone props. The current kit merges them (section 1A), so read them as notes on the mechanisms; the part sizes are in the tables above. The ESP32 bays changed on 2026-11-19: see the *Microcontroller* note in section 1.

1. **Relic Centre Connector with No Base Plates (`relic-*.stl` + `relic-token-pocket.stl`)**
   - None of the 6 Relic miniatures (`r1`–`r6`) carry a `33 mm` circular base plate. Each Relic has a flat `Ø14.6 mm` foot (`> 100 mm²` of bed contact at `z = 0`, a short 45° corbel into the sculpture) with a `Ø6.1 × 3.3 mm` socket in the middle, and it drops onto the **`Ø5.6 mm` peg** in the middle of `relic-token-pocket.stl`. The peg is small on purpose: the `hw:r1`–`hw:r6` QR codes are Version 1, error level Q (25%), and a `Ø6 mm` hole punched in the middle of the `36 × 36 mm` sticker still scans (tested with OpenCV: 6 of 6 at 6, 6.5 and 7 mm, even 0.6 mm off centre; 0 of 6 with the earlier `Ø14 mm` hole). A mounted Relic covers the sticker, so lift it off to scan.
2. **Prop #1 (`prop-01-ghost-in-box`): Coaxial Hinge + `-Y`-Shaft SG90 `XZ` Pop-Up**
   - `prop-01-chest-body.stl` mounts its SG90 horizontally on an internal pedestal at `X = -43, Y = +16.75` (`3.5 mm` front wall, flange slot centered `5.25 mm` behind case front), with the output spline pointing along **`-Y`** at `X = -48.5, Z = 32.0 mm` so `prop-01-lever-arm.stl` sweeps vertically in the **`XZ` plane** (`Y = -11.0 .. -6.5 mm` arm, stepped `11.5 mm` horn boss reaching `Y = +0.5 mm` outside the left U-track), cleanly in front of the ghost side-edge U-tracks (`X = ±36.15, Y = -4.9 .. +2.7 mm`). Its Stage-1 cam nose pushes the underside lid ramp while its `4.4 mm` slotted fork lifts `prop-01-ghost-figure.stl`'s root-chamfered `-Y` follower pin (`Y_world = -15.6 mm`, mushroom-capped return-band anchor on the floor). Recommended servo travel is **`-20°` (closed) to `+22°..+25°` (open)** (`32–34 mm` lift, keeping the ghost's dome inside the `Z = 70.0` chest rim; the nose hits the left wall past `~50°`). When `prop-01-chest-lid.stl` is flipped right-way up (`FLIP = rotate([180,0,0])`), both teardropped hinge knuckle axes sit coaxially behind the rear wall at `Y = +51.2, Z = 69.0 mm` with `0.30 mm` (`clear_move`) rim clearance (`Z_lid_rim = 70.3 mm`), and the floor ESP32 cradle (`9 mm` standoffs + `4× M2` bosses) aligns with the `45°`-gabled rear USB-C port.

## 5. Pepper's-Ghost Acetate Cutting Guide (`pyramid-holder.stl`)

To complete the Pepper's-ghost display (`pyramid-holder.stl`), cut **four identical trapezoids** from `0.3 mm`–`0.5 mm` clear overhead-projector (OHP) acetate film or clear PETG sheet:

- **Bottom edge (`baseMm`):** `60.0 mm`
- **Top edge (`topMm`):** `10.0 mm`
- **Slant height (`slantHeightMm`, 45° wall angle):** `35.36 mm`
- **Vertical height (`verticalHeightMm`):** `25.0 mm`

Slide the four trapezoids into the `45°` corner channels of `pyramid-holder.stl` (a tiny dab of clear tape along the outside seam is optional) and rest the frame over the `/party/display` calibration square on a phone or tablet.
